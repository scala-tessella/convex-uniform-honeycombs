The 13 core cells: face lattices and rigidity (Lemma A.2 of the paper)
vertices from classical coordinates; faces as maximal coplanar supporting vertex sets; flags =
incident (vertex, edge, face) triples; |Aut| = combinatorial automorphisms of the face lattice
(flag images of a base flag whose flag-graph walk closes into a bijection); |Sym| = isometries
carrying the vertex set onto itself, counted as flag images with their parity. |Aut| = |Sym|
means every combinatorial automorphism is an isometry; the 13 face-size multisets are pairwise
distinct, so the 13 face lattices are pairwise non-isomorphic.

== The cell rigidity lemma: a placed core cell is determined by its type, one edge, the two face
germs at that edge and their ROLES (orbits of faces under the symmetry group) ==
roles = number of face orbits; by size = faces of equal size share an orbit (false only for the
rco, whose 18 squares split into 6 axial and 12 belt); bisector = the reflection in the
perpendicular bisector plane of every edge is a symmetry; transitive = the symmetry group is
transitive on the (edge, ordered pair of incident faces) triples of each ordered pair of roles.
Without the roles the lemma fails: at a 4·4 edge of the rco the reflection in the plane bisecting
the dihedral angle swaps the axial and the belt square, preserving both germs, and is no symmetry.
